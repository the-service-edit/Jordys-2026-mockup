Before you book

How many people can I book for online?

Up to 16. For anything larger, email relax@jordyscasuarina.com and we’ll sort it properly rather than squeezing it through a form.

Is it alright with kids?

It’s built for them. Outdoor tables, the playground right there, and nobody minding if they’re loud. You’re not cooking tonight.

What are Happy Nights?

Wednesday and Thursday, 5–9pm: $10 margaritas, beer and wine with any pizza. No booking code, no catch — just turn up on the night.

Can we just walk in?

★VERIFY with Jordan — walk-in policy. Booking is the safe play on a Friday or Saturday, but this needs a real answer for the other nights.

Something’s changed — how do I move or cancel?

Your confirmation email from Jordy’s carries the link. If you can’t find it, call +61 431 471 911 — letting us know beats not turning up, especially on a Saturday.

More than sixteen

Birthdays, work things, the whole street.

Sixteen is as far as the online form goes. Anything bigger, tell us what you’re planning and we’ll build it around you rather than around a drop-down.
